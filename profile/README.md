# Currenari Lab

Currenari Lab is an independent lab for technical development and documentation.

Work hosted here spans electronics, firmware, technology, and design. Projects are developed in the open and documented as work progresses. Some remain exploratory. Others become complete systems, references, or tools.

The Lab also publishes open-source Ghost themes and site infrastructure used to support its own publishing work.

There is no fixed scope or roadmap. Repositories reflect what is being worked on at the time. Licensing is defined per project.

---

## Areas of work

- Hardware and open-hardware designs  
- Firmware and embedded systems  
- Technical documentation and long-form writing  
- Ghost themes and site infrastructure  
- Supporting experiments and side projects  

---

## Projects

### Spectrum One

ESP32-based WiFi RSSI activity monitor.

Open-hardware reference design intended for visualisation and comparison of nearby WiFi activity. Not a calibrated RF measurement device.

- OSHWA certified: UK000086  
- Schematics, PCB files, and firmware included  
- Hardware and firmware released under open licences  

Repository:  
https://github.com/currenari/spectrum-one
